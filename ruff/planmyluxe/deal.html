Excursion Included: Romantic Sunset Cruise with Unlimited Drinks

Set off on a memorable 45-minute sunset boat trip from the charming harbour of Cavtat. As golden hour unfolds, cruise across the tranquil waters of Župa Bay, soaking in stunning Adriatic views and the beauty of the surrounding coastline. Enjoy unlimited drinks on board, adding a touch of indulgence to this relaxing experience.

Take in panoramic scenes of picturesque coastal villages framed by lush greenery and shimmering sea—perfect for capturing unforgettable moments. Pass by Mlini, known for its historic watermills and peaceful promenade, and Srebreno, where a vibrant atmosphere meets traditional Dalmatian charm along its popular pebble beach.
